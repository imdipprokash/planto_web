I audited the publicly accessible pages of plantoapp.info against AWS's current 2026 Activate guidance. AWS says applicants need a fully functioning company website, accurate startup information, consistent business-domain information, and a linked AWS account; AWS also says it may use automated collection of publicly available website information when evaluating an application.

My main finding: the site is functional, but there are several credibility/consistency issues I would fix before submitting another Activate application. The biggest one is not design—it is internal contradictions and claims that AWS could potentially verify against your app/account.

1. Homepage — 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY

Open Planto homepage

The homepage is reasonably structured and clearly explains what Planto does. It has navigation to Features, How It Works, Pricing, Blog, FAQ, About, Careers, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Cookie Policy and Licenses.

That's good for AWS because a reviewer can understand the product without having to guess what the startup does.

The problem: too many claims

The homepage currently says:

2,999+ monthly users
500+ plants identified
99% accuracy
500+ daily identifications
4.8 average rating
50+ countries
10,000+ species
millions of training images
results under 2 seconds

These appear directly on the public homepage.

The 99% accuracy claim is especially problematic because your Features and FAQ pages say 95% accuracy, not 99%.

Change this

Do not try to make the numbers look impressive for AWS.

Make them accurate and internally consistent.

For example, if your real measured accuracy is 95%, use:

95% identification accuracy across our tested plant dataset

rather than:

99% accuracy

And only publish the 2,999-user, 500-identification, 4.8-rating and 50-country numbers if you can actually substantiate them.

Testimonials

The homepage contains testimonials attributed to:

Sarah Johnson — Home Gardener
Michael Chen — Landscape Designer
Emma Davis — Plant Enthusiast.

If these are real customers, fine.

If these are sample/generated testimonials, remove them before applying.

A public reviewer seeing generic-looking names and highly polished testimonials that cannot be verified could create an unnecessary credibility question.

Recommended homepage changes

Keep:

Product explanation
Screenshots
Features
How it works
Google Play CTA
Legal links

Fix:

99% → actual verified accuracy
Make all user/usage statistics accurate
Remove fake/sample testimonials
Add company/business identity
Add support@plantoapp.info
Consider adding a simple "About the company" section

Here:
